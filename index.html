About the Author

Taslima Tabassum Shoshy writes children's stories, dark romance and science fiction, and makes animated videos — all from Kushtia, Bangladesh. Her books live on Gumroad, Payhip and Amazon, and her videos live on YouTube as Moonlight Dreams Alone.

She also runs Twilight Digital Marketing, helping small businesses get found online with SEO, social media, content and websites.
